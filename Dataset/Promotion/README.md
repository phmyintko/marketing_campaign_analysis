# Synthetic Data
This project simulates realistic synthetic promotion data for analytics practice, SQL testing, and marketing portfolio projects.

## Long-Term Simulation
> 💡 The randomness is locked at **seed 92**, creating a permanent save point that keeps IDs and relationships consistent for ERDs, joins, and relational databases. This ensures that the dataset to evolve consistently over time.

---

## Core Features
* **Promotion Timeline Logic:** Promotion periods are intentionally shorter than campaign periods. Promotions start 3 days after campaign launch and end 2 days before campaign completion to allow for awareness-building and campaign wrap-up activities.

## Limitation
* The dataset currently covers campaign and promotion activity through May 2026. Campaigns scheduled after this date, including the December 2026 `New Year Bedroom Refresh` campaign and `New Year Mattress Discount` promotion, are not included in the dataset.
